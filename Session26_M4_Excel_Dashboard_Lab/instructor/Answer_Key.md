| Inventory | Title row + blank row above the header; TOTAL row at the bottom | Detect header row; drop TOTAL row (otherwise totals double!) |

| Feedback | CSV, not Excel; 19 blank ratings; dates as text | CSV upload works; blanks ignored in average (not counted as 0) |

## Stories hidden in the data (good answers in the Insights section)
- August revenue nearly doubles (Raksha Bandhan sweets); Sweets = ~53% of revenue.
- Surat achieved only ~76% of target – every other city is near or above 100%.
- Quick Commerce is small but growing almost every month (Rs 7.5k in Apr → Rs 28k in Aug/Sep).
- WhatsApp ROAS ~11x vs Print ~0.4x and YouTube ~0.5x – move budget.
- Own Website has the lowest rating, driven by "Delivery delay" complaints.
- 12 SKUs are at/below reorder level – 5 of them in Surat WH (incl. all 3 festive sweets).
- NPS is barely positive (~1) – almost as many detractors as promoters.

## Common student mistakes
- Inventory total units (Opening / Sold / Closing) are doubled and a "TOTAL" bar appears → TOTAL row was not removed.
- Revenue a little too high → duplicates not removed. Too low → "Rs 1,250" text rows became 0/NaN.
- Ahmedabad split into 3 bars → city spellings not standardised.
- Average rating too low → blank ratings counted as 0.
- Dates in the wrong month → text dates parsed month-first (01-04-2026 must be 1 April).
- Old chart still visible / "Canvas is already in use" error → Chart.js chart not destroyed before redraw.
